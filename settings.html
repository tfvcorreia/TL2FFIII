Sync Schedule

Transactions are automatically synced using a cron schedule.

Current Schedule: {{ sync_schedule }}

This means: Every 6 hours

To change the schedule, update the SYNC_SCHEDULE environment variable in your Docker configuration and restart the container.

Common Cron Schedules:

  • 0 */6 * * * - Every 6 hours (default)
  • 0 */12 * * * - Every 12 hours
  • 0 0 * * * - Daily at midnight
  • 0 6 * * * - Daily at 6 AM
  • 0 */4 * * * - Every 4 hours
